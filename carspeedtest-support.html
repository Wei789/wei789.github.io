CarSpeedTest 支援

用手機 GPS 與加速度計測量 0–100 km/h 等加速時間。

在 App Store 下載 CarSpeedTest

快速上手

  1. 把手機固定在車架上，打開 App。
  2. 等右上角顯示「GPS 良好」。
  3. 點 Start 上方的「0–100 km/h」可以換目標速度。
  4. 車子停妥後按 Start，起步就開始計時，到達目標速度自動停止並儲存成績。
  5. 按 X 關閉成績；左上角選單可看最近 10 筆成績與速度曲線。

常見問題

按 Start 後計時沒有開始？

計時要等車子真的動起來。請確認已允許定位、右上角不是「GPS 定位中」，並在空曠處使用。

為什麼要定位權限？

車速來自 GPS。第一次按 Start 時才會詢問；位置只在手機上計算，不會上傳。拒絕後可到「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 定位服務 › CarSpeedTest」開啟。

成績準確嗎？

起點由加速度計偵測起步瞬間，終點在兩筆 GPS 資料之間計算。手機要固定在車架上，在訊號良好、視野開闊的地方測量最準。手機 GPS 的精度仍不如專用的 GPS 計時器。

測到一半停下來會怎樣？

速度降回 0 或 GPS 訊號中斷時，計時會從頭開始，等下一次起步。

怎麼換 km/h 或 mph？語音報成績在哪裡開？

點左上角的齒輪進入設定，可以切換速度單位與「語音報成績」。

為什麼有些舊成績沒有 🏆？

3.1 改進了計時方式，只有用同一種方式、同一個目標速度與單位測的成績會互相比較。

換手機後紀錄還在嗎？

登入同一個 iCloud 帳號時，最近 10 筆成績與設定會自動同步。

怎麼刪除成績？

在「最近紀錄」把該筆成績向左滑並刪除，iCloud 裡的同一筆也會刪除。

需要登入或付費嗎？

不需要帳號，App 免費，以廣告支持。測速過程中不會跳出廣告。

CarSpeedTest Support

Time your 0–100 km/h (and more) runs with your phone's GPS and motion sensor.

Get CarSpeedTest on the App Store

Getting started

  1. Put your phone in a car mount and open the app.
  2. Wait until the top right shows "GPS good".
  3. Tap "0–100 km/h" above Start to change the target speed.
  4. With the car stopped, tap Start. Timing begins when you pull away and stops and saves at the target speed.
  5. Tap X to close the result. The menu at the top left shows your latest 10 results and their speed curves.

FAQ

I tapped Start but the timer doesn't run.

Timing begins once the car actually moves. Make sure location access is allowed, the top right doesn't say "Finding GPS…", and you have a clear view of the sky.

Why does the app need location access?

Your speed comes from GPS. The app asks only when you first tap Start, and your location never leaves your phone. If you declined, turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › CarSpeedTest.

How accurate are the times?

The start comes from the motion sensor catching the launch, and the finish is calculated between GPS readings. Mount the phone firmly and test where the GPS signal is good. Phone GPS is still less precise than a dedicated GPS timer.

What happens if I stop mid-run?

If the speed drops to 0 or GPS is lost, timing starts over and waits for the next launch.

How do I switch between km/h and mph, or turn on Speak Result?

Tap the gear at the top left to open Settings.

Why don't some older results get a 🏆?

Version 3.1 improved how runs are timed, so only results timed the same way, with the same target and unit, are compared.

Will my results move to a new iPhone?

Yes. When you're signed in to the same iCloud account, your latest 10 results and settings sync automatically.

How do I delete a result?

Swipe left on it in Recent record and tap Delete. It is also removed from iCloud.

Do I need an account or to pay?

No account is needed. The app is free and supported by ads, which never appear while you are timing a run.
