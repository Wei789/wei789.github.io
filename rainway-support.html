App Support & FAQ

RainWay is built for commuters and parents who travel between multiple places every day. It displays the highest chance of rain at each of your daily stops (home, school, office) during your morning and evening commute windows, and tells you in one line whether to bring rain gear.

Getting Started

  1. Set up your stops: Tap the gear button at the top right ➔ Commute Stops ➔ Add Stop, then search for any district, city, or landmark. Tap a stop to edit its nickname and icon; tap Edit to drag and reorder. At least 2 stops are required.
  2. Set your commute hours: In Commute Hours, choose your outbound and return windows (default 07:00–09:00 and 17:00–19:00). Both start and end hours are included.
  3. Morning alerts: Turn on Daily Morning Alert, pick an alert time and weekdays, and choose Every day or Only when rain. A preview of your next scheduled alert is shown right below.
  4. Add the Home Screen widget: Touch and hold an empty area on your Home Screen ➔ tap Edit or + ➔ search for RainWay ➔ select the Small or Medium Commute Rain widget ➔ tap Add Widget.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q1: How often is the weather forecast updated?
    The app checks for fresh data whenever you open it, close settings, or pull to refresh (forecasts downloaded within the last 30 minutes are reused). The Home Screen widget refreshes roughly once per hour as scheduled by iOS.
  • Q2: What happens when I am offline?
    The app displays the last cached forecast along with its "Updated" timestamp (including the date if it was fetched on a previous day). If the widget's cached forecast is more than 12 hours old, it displays "Open RainWay to load the forecast".
  • Q3: Why didn't I receive a morning alert?
    • Check that notifications are enabled in iOS Settings ➔ RainWay ➔ Notifications.
    • In Only when rain mode, RainWay stays silent when every stop is below 20% rain probability in both commute windows.
    • Alerts are scheduled locally up to 3 days ahead whenever you open the app or the widget refreshes. Keeping the widget on your Home Screen helps keep your alerts continuously scheduled.
  • Q4: Why did an alert say the forecast is unavailable?
    RainWay never relies on forecasts older than 12 hours for morning alerts and never assumes a day is dry if any stop lacks data. Simply open the app to refresh.
  • Q5: Why does the main screen say "Some stops have no forecast yet"?
    One or more of your stops hasn't downloaded weather data yet (for example, you added a stop while offline). Connect to the internet and pull down on the main screen to refresh.
  • Q6: Why doesn't RainWay ask for Location permission or an account?
    Stops are added manually via search so you can check weather across all your destinations without draining battery on background GPS or creating an account.
  • Q7: How do I delete my data?
    All stops, settings, and cached forecasts are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the RainWay app removes all data immediately.
