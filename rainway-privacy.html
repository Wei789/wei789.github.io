Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026
App: RainWay (通勤知雨)
Developer: CowCow

1. Information We Collect

  • By the Developer: None. RainWay has no user accounts and no backend servers of our own. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers.
  • By Third-Party SDKs: The Google Mobile Ads SDK integrated for the bottom banner ad in the main app may collect certain device and interaction data as described in Section 6.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored & Retention

Your saved stops (nickname, icon, and coordinates), commute windows, morning alert settings, and cached forecasts are stored exclusively on your iPhone in shared App Group storage (UserDefaults) used by the app and its Home Screen widget. They remain on your device until you edit them or uninstall the app; deleting the app permanently removes all on-device data.

3. System Permissions

  • Notifications: Requested only when you enable Daily Morning Alert in settings, used solely to schedule local notifications on your device. No remote push server is used.
  • Location: Never requested. You add commute stops manually via place search; RainWay never accesses your GPS location.
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): Requested on first launch. If you allow tracking, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may access your advertising identifier (IDFA) to deliver more relevant ads; if you select "Ask App Not to Track", IDFA is not accessed and all app and widget features remain fully available. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings ➔ Privacy & Security ➔ Tracking. Your commute stops and preferences are never shared for tracking.

4. Network Connections & Third-Party Services

  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com): Receives the coordinates of your saved stops and your device's time zone to return hourly weather forecasts. No personal identifiers are sent. Open-Meteo Terms & Privacy
  • Apple Maps (MapKit MKLocalSearch): Receives the text query you type when searching for a new stop. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: See Section 6.

5. Home Screen Widget

The Home Screen widget reads your on-device settings, fetches hourly forecasts from Open-Meteo, and refreshes your local morning alert schedule for the next 3 days. The widget contains no ads.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The main screen displays one banner ad served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). According to Google's disclosures, the SDK may collect your IP address (and coarse location derived from it), device identifiers, product and ad interaction data, and crash/performance diagnostics for advertising, measurement, and analytics. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you grant permission via the ATT prompt. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

RainWay is a general-audience weather utility (rated 4+) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can edit or remove stops at any time in the app, revoke notification or tracking permissions in iOS Settings, and delete all stored data by uninstalling the RainWay app. Because we store no data on our own servers, there is no server-side profile to delete.

9. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at Kaishacowcow@gmail.com or visit our App Support page.
