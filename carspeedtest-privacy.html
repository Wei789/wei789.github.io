CarSpeedTest 隱私權政策

開發者：Cow Cow　生效日期：2026 年 11 月 9 日

我們收集的資料

開發者不收集、也不傳送任何個人資料到我們的伺服器。我們沒有伺服器，也沒有帳號系統。

第三方收集的資料（廣告）

App 使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。Google 可能收集：

  • 大略位置（由 IP 位址推估）
  • 裝置識別碼（廣告識別碼 IDFA，僅在你於「允許追蹤」對話框同意時）
  • 產品互動與看過的廣告
  • 當機與效能資料

這些資料用於投放與衡量廣告。第一次開啟 App 時，iOS 會詢問是否允許追蹤；拒絕後仍會顯示廣告，但不會使用廣告識別碼做個人化。你可以隨時在「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 追蹤」更改。詳見 Google 隱私權政策 與 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊。

權限與感測器

  • 定位（使用 App 期間）：用 GPS 測量車速並計時。第一次按 Start 時才會詢問。位置與速度只在你的裝置上計算，不會上傳。
  • 動作感測器（加速度計）：偵測車子起步的瞬間，不需要權限，資料不離開裝置。
  • App 不要求相機、麥克風、相簿、聯絡人、通知或健康資料權限。

資料存在哪裡、怎麼刪除

  • 最近 10 筆成績（時間、速度曲線、目標速度、單位、日期）與設定存在你的裝置上。
  • 登入 iCloud 時，成績與設定會透過你自己的 iCloud 帳號同步到你的其他裝置。我們無法讀取這些資料。
  • 在「最近紀錄」向左滑可刪除單筆成績，iCloud 裡的同一筆也會一起刪除。刪除 App 會清除裝置上的資料。

連線的服務

  • Google AdMob：廣告請求（見上方說明）。
  • Apple iCloud：同步成績與設定。
  • App Store：「更多 App」會開啟我們其他 App 的 App Store 頁面。

兒童隱私

本 App 分級為 4+，不針對兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

你的權利與聯絡方式

因為我們不保存你的個人資料，沒有可供查詢或刪除的開發者端資料。對廣告資料的請求，請依 Google 隱私權政策辦理。有任何問題請寄信到 kaishacowcow@gmail.com。

政策變更

政策變更時會更新本頁並修改生效日期；重大變更會在 App 的版本更新說明中告知。

CarSpeedTest Privacy Policy

Developer: Cow Cow　Effective date: November 9, 2026

Data we collect

We do not collect or send any personal data to our servers. We run no servers and have no accounts.

Data collected by third parties (ads)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect:

  • Coarse location (estimated from your IP address)
  • Device identifier (the advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking)
  • Product interaction and the ads you have seen
  • Crash and performance data

This data is used to serve and measure ads. When you first open the app, iOS asks whether to allow tracking. If you decline, ads still appear but the advertising identifier is not used to personalize them. You can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

Permissions and sensors

  • Location (While Using the App): measures your speed with GPS and times your run. It is requested only when you first tap Start. Location and speed are processed on your device and never uploaded.
  • Motion sensor (accelerometer): detects the moment the car pulls away. It needs no permission and the data never leaves your device.
  • The app does not request camera, microphone, photos, contacts, notifications or health data.

Where your data is stored and how to delete it

  • Your latest 10 results (time, speed curve, target speed, unit, date) and your settings are stored on your device.
  • When you are signed in to iCloud, results and settings sync to your other devices through your own iCloud account. We cannot read this data.
  • Swipe left on a result in Recent record to delete it; the same result is also removed from iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on your device.

Services the app connects to

  • Google AdMob: ad requests (see above).
  • Apple iCloud: syncing results and settings.
  • App Store: More Apps opens the App Store pages of our other apps.

Children's privacy

The app is rated 4+. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights and contact

Because we keep no personal data, there is nothing on our side to access or delete. For ad data, follow the Google Privacy Policy. Questions: kaishacowcow@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when the policy changes, and mention major changes in the app's release notes.
