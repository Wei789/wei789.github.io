Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I add the RainWay widget to my Home Screen?
    Touch and hold an empty area on your iPhone Home Screen until the apps jiggle ➔ Tap the "+" or "Edit" button in the upper-left corner ➔ Search for and select "RainWay" ➔ Choose the Small (2x2) or Medium (4x2) size and tap "Add Widget".
  • Why didn't I receive my morning rain notification?
    If you enabled the "Notify Only When Rain is Expected" option, RainWay will stay silent on clear days when rain probabilities across all your stops are below 30%. Also, please ensure notifications are enabled under iOS Settings ➔ RainWay ➔ Notifications.
  • Does RainWay track my live GPS location or drain battery?
    No! RainWay does not require "Always Allow" GPS location permissions. You simply search and save your routine commute stops manually, ensuring zero background GPS battery drain and complete location privacy.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

RainWay respects and protects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our iOS application and widgets.

1. Data Collection & Local Storage

  • No Account or Personal Identity Required: RainWay does not require user registration. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.
  • No Live GPS Tracking: We do not request or record your continuous real-time GPS location. All commute stops, geographic coordinates, time windows, and notification preferences you configure are stored strictly locally on your iOS device. We do not operate any cloud servers or databases to store your personal route data.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

  • Weather Data Providers: To provide accurate weather forecasts, the app sends only the geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) of your saved stops directly to third-party weather APIs (such as Open-Meteo or Apple WeatherKit). These requests contain no personally identifiable information.
  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display a standard banner advertisement at the bottom of the main app interface (Home Screen widgets remain 100% ad-free). Depending on your iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission settings, Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as IDFA), coarse location data (derived from IP address), product interaction data, and crash/diagnostic logs for serving ads and analytics. For more details, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Your Choices & Control

You can manage or revoke ad tracking permissions at any time in your device's Settings ➔ Privacy & Security ➔ Tracking. You may also edit or delete your saved locations within the app at any time. Uninstalling RainWay from your device will permanently remove all locally stored settings.

4. Contact Us
