BabyCam 寶寶監視器

把閒置的 iPhone 或 iPad 變成寶寶監視器，畫面與聲音只在你家的 Wi‑Fi 裡直接傳送，不經雲端、不用帳號。

在 App Store 搜尋「BabyCam」下載。

快速上手

  1. 兩台裝置連上同一個 Wi‑Fi。
  2. 放在寶寶身邊的那台打開 App，選「寶寶端」，允許相機、麥克風與區域網路。畫面會顯示 4 位數配對碼。
  3. 另一台選「父母端」，允許區域網路，輸入寶寶端的配對碼。
  4. 連上後就能看到畫面、聽到聲音。按住「按住說話」可以對寶寶說話。
  5. 之後兩台打開 App 會自動重新連線，不用再輸入配對碼。

常見問題

需要網路或帳號嗎？

兩台裝置要在同一個 Wi‑Fi，但不需要能連上網際網路，也不用註冊帳號或登入。也不需要定位權限。

畫面會上傳到雲端或被錄下來嗎？

不會。畫面與聲音只在你的兩台裝置之間直接傳送並加密，App 不錄影、不儲存。詳見隱私權政策。

找不到寶寶端、一直顯示「正在尋找寶寶端…」

請確認：兩台在同一個 Wi‑Fi（不是一台用行動網路）；兩台都允許了「區域網路」（設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 區域網路 › BabyCam）；寶寶端的 App 開著。部分公共或飯店 Wi‑Fi 會阻擋裝置互連，這時無法使用。

配對碼輸入錯誤，或顯示已停止配對

配對碼只能用一次，連續輸錯 3 次寶寶端會停止配對以防被猜中。請在寶寶端按「配對新裝置」取得新的配對碼。

沒有收到聲音提醒或斷線通知

通知會在父母端回到主畫面或鎖定螢幕時出現；App 開在前景時不跳通知，畫面上的狀態會顯示「有聲音」。父母端第一次開始觀看時會詢問通知權限，請允許；若之前拒絕，到「設定 › 通知 › BabyCam」開啟。也請確認父母端右上角選單「提醒設定」裡的開關有打開。聲音要持續超過 5 秒才會通知，可調高靈敏度；觀看中偵測到斷線後再過 10 秒仍未恢復，才會發出斷線通知。

斷線後會自己連回來嗎？

會。父母端會持續自動重新連線，寶寶端恢復後畫面就會回來。

寶寶端螢幕變暗了，還在傳送嗎？

是。寶寶端 30 秒後會把螢幕調到最暗，避免亮光吵到寶寶，傳送不會停止。點一下螢幕即可喚醒，也可以選「保持亮著」。寶寶端請保持 App 開啟，長時間使用請接上電源。

切到其他 App 還能看嗎？

可以。觀看中回到主畫面會自動進入子母畫面。

另一台父母端顯示「另一台裝置正在觀看」

一台寶寶端同一時間只給一台父母端觀看。在這台按「改由這台觀看」即可接手。

怎麼刪除配對資料？

在寶寶端右上角選單選「清除已配對裝置」，正在連線的父母端會一起清掉配對；其他父母端下次連線會被拒絕。之後要重新輸入配對碼。

BabyCam Baby Monitor

Turn a spare iPhone or iPad into a baby monitor. Video and sound travel directly over your home Wi-Fi, with no cloud and no account.

Search for “BabyCam” on the App Store.

Please note: the app interface is in Traditional Chinese. Chinese labels are shown below with their English meaning.

Getting started

  1. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. On the device next to your baby, open the app, choose 寶寶端 (Baby unit) and allow Camera, Microphone and Local Network. A 4-digit pairing code appears.
  3. On the other device, choose 父母端 (Parent unit), allow Local Network and enter the pairing code.
  4. Once connected you can see and hear your baby. Hold 按住說話 (Hold to talk) to talk to your baby.
  5. From then on the devices reconnect automatically; no need to enter the code again.

FAQ

Do I need internet or an account?

Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network, but no internet connection, account or sign-in is needed. Location access is not needed either.

Is video uploaded to the cloud or recorded?

No. Video and sound go directly and encrypted between your two devices. The app does not record or store anything. See the Privacy Policy.

The parent unit keeps searching for the baby unit

Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi (not mobile data), that both allow Local Network (Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › BabyCam), and that the app is open on the baby unit. Some public or hotel Wi-Fi networks block devices from connecting to each other.

Wrong pairing code, or pairing stopped

Each code works once. After 3 wrong codes the baby unit stops pairing so the code cannot be guessed. Tap 配對新裝置 (Pair new device) on the baby unit to get a new code.

I don't get sound or disconnection alerts

Notifications appear when the parent unit is on the Home Screen or locked. While the app is open it shows 有聲音 (sound) on screen instead of a notification. The parent unit asks for notification permission when you first start watching. If you declined, turn it on in Settings › Notifications › BabyCam. Also check the switches under 提醒設定 (Alert settings) in the parent unit's top-right menu. A sound must last more than 5 seconds to trigger an alert; you can raise the sensitivity. A disconnection alert is sent when the connection is still down 10 seconds after a dropout is detected while you are watching.

Does it reconnect after a dropout?

Yes. The parent unit keeps reconnecting automatically, and the picture returns once the baby unit is back.

The baby unit's screen went dark. Is it still sending?

Yes. After 30 seconds the baby unit dims its screen to the lowest brightness so the light does not disturb your baby; streaming continues. Tap the screen to wake it, or choose 保持亮著 (Keep screen on). Keep the app open on the baby unit and plug it in for long sessions.

Can I keep watching in another app?

Yes. Going to the Home Screen while watching starts Picture in Picture automatically.

Another parent unit shows "another device is watching"

A baby unit streams to one parent unit at a time. Tap 改由這台觀看 (Watch on this device) to take over.

How do I delete pairing data?

On the baby unit, open the top-right menu and choose 清除已配對裝置 (Remove paired devices). A connected parent unit is cleared too; other parent units are refused on their next connection. All of them will need a new pairing code.
